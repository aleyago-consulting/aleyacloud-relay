# Relay delivery roadmap

## Phase 0 — Architecture and readiness

- Keep the public landing separate from the private application.
- Confirm authentication/MFA, Meta App ownership, privacy/retention and the
  deployment baseline.
- Freeze the current prototype as a foundation pending the Workspace/Brand
  migration.

## Phase 1 — Social MVP

- Workspace, Brand, Membership and roles with strict tenant isolation.
- Meta OAuth, explicit asset selection, encrypted credentials, renewal and
  errors for Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional Accounts.
- Editorial calendar, campaigns, drafts, variants, one-image media and
  brand-specific voice/guide/restrictions for human-reviewed AI assistance.
- Client approval links, comments and requested changes.
- Reliable scheduled publication, state tracking, bounded retries, audit and
  failure alerts.
- Content panel with publications, reach, interactions, UTM clicks and failures.
- UTM templates, publication metrics, aggregated attribution, consent and
  security controls.

## Phase 2 — Commercial conversion minimum

Designed during Phase 1 but not implemented until the Social MVP is validated:

- Unified lead capture from forms, Meta Lead Ads, Instagram, email and Google
  Business.
- Minimal opportunity, conversation, owner, appointment and outcome workflow.
- Consent-bound email/WhatsApp follow-up, no-show recovery and alerts.
- Human approval for sensitive AI-generated messages.
- Attribution from publication/campaign/UTM to visit, lead, appointment and sale.
- One approved vertical template: real estate, fitness/wellbeing or another
  single Direction-approved vertical.

This is not a general CRM or generic automation system.

## After Phase 2

1. Google Business publishing/insights, where not already used for capture.
2. LinkedIn.
3. TikTok.
4. Rich media: carousels, video and Reels.
5. Advanced approvals and expanded analytics.

No additional network launches before the Meta workflow is stable, approvable and
measurable.
